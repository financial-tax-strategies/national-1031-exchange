## Risk Analysis & Mitigation

### Primary Risks

1. **Manual Content Trap:** Creating assets before infrastructure = inefficient scaling
2. **Quality Degradation:** Rapid scaling without quality controls
3. **Integration Complexity:** Technical challenges with existing systems

### Mitigation Strategies

- Infrastructure-first approach
- Rigorous quality gates at each phase
- Incremental rollout with testing

## Services Offered
1. **Delayed Exchange** (Forward Exchange)
   - Standard 1031 exchange process
   - Sell property first, then buy replacement
   - 45-day identification period
   - 180-day exchange completion deadline

2. **Reverse Exchange**
   - Buy replacement property first
   - Ideal for competitive markets
   - Exchange Accommodation Titleholder (EAT) structure
   - 180-day total completion period

3. **Improvement Exchange** (Build-to-Suit)
   - Use exchange funds for property improvements
   - Build or improve replacement property
   - Must complete within 180 days
   - Requires careful timing and coordination

4. **Partial Exchange**
   - Strategic cash-out while deferring most taxes
   - Defer taxes on majority of proceeds
   - Flexible investment strategies
   - Balance cash needs with tax benefits

## Important 1031 Exchange Rules
- Like-kind property requirement (real estate for real estate)
- Equal or greater value rule for full tax deferral
- 45-day identification deadline (strict)
- 180-day exchange completion deadline (strict)
- Three property rule (identify up to 3 properties)
- 200% rule (identify any number if total value ≤200% of sold property)
- 95% rule (identify any amount if purchase ≥95% of identified value)
- No direct receipt of funds (must use qualified intermediary)

## Common Client Questions
**Q: What is a 1031 exchange?**
A: A tax strategy allowing real estate investors to defer capital gains taxes by exchanging investment property for like-kind property.

**Q: What types of property qualify?**
A: Most real estate held for investment or business use qualifies, including rental properties, commercial buildings, land, and multi-family properties.

**Q: Can I live in the property?**
A: No, properties must be held for investment or business purposes, not personal residence.

**Q: What are the deadlines?**
A: 45 days to identify replacement property, 180 days to complete the exchange.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: Exchange fees vary based on transaction complexity. Contact us for a custom quote.

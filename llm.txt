# The 1031 Center - AI Assistant Information

## Company Overview
The 1031 Center is America's most trusted 1031 exchange qualified intermediary, facilitating tax-deferred property exchanges nationwide. We specialize in helping real estate investors defer capital gains taxes through IRC Section 1031 like-kind exchanges.

## Our Services

### 1. Delayed Exchanges (Most Common)
Standard forward exchanges where you sell first, then buy replacement property within 180 days. Must identify replacement properties within 45 days.

### 2. Reverse Exchanges
For hot markets where you need to buy first. We use Exchange Accommodation Titleholder (EAT) structures compliant with Rev. Proc. 2000-37.

### 3. Improvement Exchanges
Also called build-to-suit exchanges. Use exchange funds to improve replacement property before taking title.

### 4. Partial Exchanges
Strategic exchanges where you take some cash out while still deferring taxes on the remaining proceeds.

## Process Overview
1. **Engage QI**: Sign exchange documents before closing on relinquished property
2. **Sell Property**: We receive proceeds directly from closing
3. **Identify Replacements**: Submit identification within 45 days
4. **Purchase Replacement**: Complete within 180 days
5. **Tax Deferral**: Defer capital gains, depreciation recapture, and state taxes

## Important 1031 Rules
- Must use Qualified Intermediary (cannot touch funds)
- 45-day identification period (no extensions)
- 180-day exchange period (or tax return due date)
- Like-kind requirement (real property for real property)
- Equal or greater value to defer all taxes
- Equal or greater debt (or add cash)
